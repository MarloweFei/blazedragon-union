Buzzle Beads AdMob 只读日报

Read-only AdMob reporting for the Buzzle Beads operations team.

本工具由 BLAZEDRAGON UNION INTERACTIVE ENTERTAINMENT LIMITED 用于内部经营分析：读取 Buzzle Beads 的 AdMob 聚合报表，并将日汇总更新到管理者指定的 Google 表格。此页面仅提供公开说明，经营数据保持受限共享。

What the tool does

  • Retrieves the configured AdMob account’s reporting timezone and currency, then requests the Buzzle Beads application’s daily mediation report.
  • Organizes impressions, clicks and estimated earnings by date, ad source and ad format.
  • Refreshes a fixed operations spreadsheet with scheduled Google Apps Script tasks. Administrators review results in Google Sheets.

The integration does not modify ads, bidding settings or payments. Report access is granted separately to named Google accounts; publishing this documentation does not publish the spreadsheet.

Google permissions

PermissionPurpose
admob.reportRead AdMob reports and reporting-account metadata.
spreadsheetsWrite reporting tables to the fixed operations spreadsheet. The Google scope is broader than one file; the implementation uses only the configured destination.
script.external_requestCall the official Google AdMob API.
script.scriptappCreate and manage this reporting script’s scheduled updates.

Who can use it

This tool is for authorized company operators. A Google account must already have the relevant AdMob and spreadsheet permissions. There is no public sign-up or public report endpoint on this site.

Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use before authorizing the tool.
