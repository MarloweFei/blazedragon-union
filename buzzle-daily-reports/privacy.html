Buzzle Beads AdMob 只读日报

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy covers the internal reporting tool operated by BLAZEDRAGON UNION INTERACTIVE ENTERTAINMENT LIMITED. It describes this tool’s handling of Google API data, separately from the Buzzle Beads game’s player-facing privacy policy.

Data accessed and purpose

With an authorized operator’s consent, the tool reads the configured AdMob publisher account identifier, reporting timezone and currency, and the configured Buzzle Beads application’s aggregate mediation reports. Report fields include reporting date, application identifier, ad-source identifiers and names, ad format, impressions, clicks and estimated earnings.

These data are used only to provide the requested daily operations reports, refresh those reports and diagnose reporting failures. This integration does not request Gmail, contacts, payment details, advertising-edit permissions or individual players’ advertising identifiers.

Storage and access

Aggregate reports are written to a fixed Google Sheets spreadsheet controlled by the administrator. Runtime settings and success/error status are held in Google Apps Script properties and execution logs. OAuth authorization is managed by Google Apps Script; the tool does not write access tokens into the spreadsheet or this website.

The spreadsheet is shared only with accounts explicitly authorized by its administrator. Authorized operators may view aggregates to manage reporting and investigate errors. Data are processed using Google’s AdMob, Apps Script and Sheets services under the applicable Google service settings.

Sharing and limited use

We do not sell Google user data, publish private reports on this site, use those data for advertising targeting, or use them to train generalized AI or machine-learning models. Data are used and shared only as necessary to provide and support the reporting functionality described here, subject to the administrator’s explicit sharing choices.

The tool’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

The collector refreshes the most recent 32 completed reporting days. Earlier aggregate rows are retained in the administrator-controlled spreadsheet until the administrator deletes them; 32 days is a refresh window, not an automatic deletion period. Runtime and platform logs follow Google’s applicable retention settings.

Administrators can delete reporting rows or the spreadsheet, remove scheduled triggers and delete the Apps Script project. To request assistance with access or deletion, contact postmaster@blazedragonsoul.xyz. Revoking authorization stops future authorized API access but does not automatically delete previously stored reports.

Control and revocation

You may review and revoke the tool’s access from your Google Account connections. Removing access may cause scheduled updates to fail until a permitted operator authorizes the tool again.

This documentation website

These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising tags, sign-in form or report data. The hosting and network providers may process routine request information needed to serve the pages. Links to Google services are governed by those services’ own policies.

Updates and contact

We will update this page if this tool’s data practices change and identify the effective date above. Support and privacy requests: postmaster@blazedragonsoul.xyz.
